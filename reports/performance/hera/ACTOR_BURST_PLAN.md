# Follow-up to the two-connection role-read burst

The first candidate had one admission lane for authentication and one for business, and verified all 1000 character reads in 51.29 s. Its business lane was fully occupied. The historical Host-queued read was 35.26 s. Hold the physical SQL pool at two, use the same two books, same 1000 independently bound SDK clients, and keep the complete task oracle. Compare optional shared authentication/business connection slots versus the split without a client limiter. In the shared variant, one common semaphore caps simultaneous short calls to two. Neither operation holds that semaphore while waiting for the other phase. Freeze A/B/B/A with the same prepared roles, capture errors and full distributions. Require both comparisons faster, no additional errors, and preserve P99 within 20% before recommending this mode. Compare elapsed seconds to the historical target separately.
