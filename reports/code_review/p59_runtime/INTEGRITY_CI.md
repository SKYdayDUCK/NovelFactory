The four failed profiles predate this branch. The 50-overload call-start rates and 1000-open successful-read rate were lower on this candidate measurement. The 200-overload P2 pair went in the opposite direction. These runs use separately assigned GitHub-hosted runners, so they do not identify a change responsible for either movement. Every 50/200 overload repeat on this pair reported correctness and bounded business recovery PASS. The 1000-open job counts first attempts only. [Selected hosted evidence](raw/hosted-capacity-comparison.json)

Two additional head measurements distinguish the lower 50-overload sample from a persistent regression. On earlier `9bc6b79`, both P2 repeats reached 237.95/242.88 call starts/s. On later `23c62ab`, they reached 228.00/278.23. Only the native Codex relay and reports changed between `ce74f26` and `23c62ab`. The later 200-overload P3/P4 measurement reached 60.82/s. All of these measurements remained below 950. In contrast, the 1000-open result stayed below the frozen main sample on both other heads, with 1821/5000 and 1837/5000 successes respectively. The three candidate runs do not establish an HTTP capacity improvement. [Additional hosted jobs](raw/additional-hosted-capacity.json)
